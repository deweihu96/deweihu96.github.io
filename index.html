PhD Fellow · NNF Center for Basic Metabolic Research, University of Copenhagen

I am a PhD fellow in Bioinformatics & Network Biology, supervised by Simon Rasmussen and Lars Juhl Jensen.

My research focuses on machine learning and network biology methods for understanding complex diseases — integrating metabolomics, proteomics, and protein interaction networks at population scale. I also develop cross-species protein embeddings built on the STRING database.
